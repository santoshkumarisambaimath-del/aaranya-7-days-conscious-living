# AARANYA – 7 Days of Conscious Living 🌿

## About the Campaign

AARANYA's "7 Days of Conscious Living" is a sustainability-focused awareness campaign designed to encourage simple, meaningful everyday choices that can positively impact our planet.

## Campaign Theme

**Small choices. Meaningful change.**

## 7-Day Challenge

### Day 1 – Start Small
Bring your own bottle. Carry a reusable bag. Make one choice that helps our planet.

### Day 2 – Reduce Waste
Reuse containers, repair items instead of replacing them, and give old items a second life.

### Day 3 – Save Water
Turn off the tap while brushing, take shorter showers, and reuse water whenever possible.

### Day 4 – Go Plastic-Light
Reduce single-use plastics and choose reusable alternatives.

### Day 5 – Choose Green
Make one eco-friendly choice such as walking, cycling, or choosing sustainable products.

### Day 6 – Connect With Nature
Spend time with nature, plant something green, and help keep outdoor spaces clean.

### Day 7 – Keep It Going
Continue the sustainable habits you started during the week and turn small actions into lasting habits.

## Goal

To encourage people to adopt simple, practical, and sustainable habits in their everyday lives.
